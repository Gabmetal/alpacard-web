## 4. Reddit — Community Founder Post (r/Coffee, r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur)

### Title
> I built a lightweight digital punch card platform for local cafes to replace paper cards (No app download required)

### Body
> Hey everyone,
> 
> Like many of you, my wallet was filled with half-stamped paper loyalty cards from different coffee shops and bakeries that I always forgot to bring when actually visiting.
> 
> When I talked to local shop owners, they told me they hated paper cards (no customer data, rampant fraud, people losing them), but they couldn't justify paying \$99/month for bloated loyalty apps that force customers to download something from the App Store.
> 
> So I spent the last few weeks building **Alpacard** (https://alpacard.gabo.lat):
> 
> **How it works:**
> 1. Store signs up, picks brand colors, uploads a logo and reward (e.g. "Buy 8 Coffees, 9th is Free").
> 2. The platform generates printable Stand QR codes.
> 3. Customers scan with their normal camera — the card opens in their browser wallet instantly.
> 4. Cashiers scan the customer's dynamic QR code with any phone or tablet to punch stamps or redeem prizes.
> 5. When a prize is redeemed, the card triggers an interactive confetti celebration with fanfare audio and resets the stamps for the next cycle.
> 
> **Technical Stack:**
> - Backend: Go + GORM + PostgreSQL + RabbitMQ.
> - Frontend: React 18 + Vite + Tailwind CSS micro-frontends.
> - Infra: Containerized with automatic nightly backups to Cloudflare R2 and Prometheus/VictoriaMetrics alerting.
> 
> It's completely free to try at https://alpacard.gabo.lat. I would love feedback from cafe owners, baristas, or frequent coffee drinkers on what features would make your daily routine smoother!
