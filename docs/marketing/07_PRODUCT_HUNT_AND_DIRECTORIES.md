### Short Description (250 chars):
> Self-hosted, lightweight digital loyalty punch card platform. Replace lost paper stamp cards and expensive $100/mo SaaS subscriptions with a fast Go backend, zero-friction customer PWA, and tamper-proof camera QR scanning.

### First Maker Comment (Copy-Paste):
> Hey Product Hunt community! 👋
> 
> Small local businesses—coffee shops, bakeries, food trucks—get squeezed by expensive proprietary SaaS tools charging $60–$150/month just to manage loyalty punch cards. Worse, these tools force customers to install heavy apps while waiting in line.
> 
> We built **Alpacard** as a free, 100% open-source alternative:
> 
> 🦙 **Zero App-Store Downloads**: Customers point their phone camera at a counter QR code, and their card opens instantly in a lightweight PWA.  
> 🔒 **Anti-Replay Fraud Protection**: Dynamic 5-minute cryptographic tokens with atomic PostgreSQL transactions prevent screenshots and duplicate stamps.  
> 🎨 **Personalization & Menus**: Custom colors, merchant logos, stamp icons, and integrated digital/PDF menus.  
> ⚡ **Ultra-Efficient**: The Go backend runs with ~20MB of RAM—easy to self-host with 1-command Docker Compose or Kubernetes.
> 
> Everything is AGPLv3 licensed and free forever for self-hosters. We’d love your feedback!
