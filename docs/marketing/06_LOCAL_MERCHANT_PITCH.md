# ☕ Local Merchant Outreach Playbook (Guerrilla Pitch)

This guide provides field-tested conversational scripts to onboard 3–5 local coffee shops, bakeries, or food trucks for free.

---

## 🎯 Target Businesses
- Specialty coffee shops
- Artisan bakeries & pastry shops
- Independent tea / boba shops
- Food trucks & pop-ups
- Barbershops & beauty salons

*(Look for stores that currently have a stack of paper stamp cards sitting by their cash register).*

---

## 🗣️ In-Person 2-Minute Elevator Pitch (Spanish)

**When**: Visit during off-peak hours (typically 3:00 PM – 4:30 PM) when the barista/manager isn’t overwhelmed. Order a coffee first!

> **Tú**: *"¡Hola! Qué rico café. Oye, una pregunta rápida: veo que usan tarjetitas de papel para los sellos. ¿Les pasa seguido que los clientes las pierden o las olvidan en casa?"*  
> **Dueño/Barista**: *"Sí, todo el tiempo. La gente siempre pide una nueva porque la dejó en otro pantalón."*  
> **Tú**: *"Claro, es súper común. Mira, soy desarrollador de software y creé una plataforma de código abierto que se llama **Alpacard**. Es una tarjeta de fidelidad digital que los clientes guardan directo en su celular sin tener que descargar ninguna app de la App Store. Simplemente escanean este código QR en el mostrador y listo.  
> Estoy haciendo un piloto con 3 cafeterías del barrio para que la usen **100% gratis para siempre**. Yo mismo les configuro el logo, sus colores y les imprimo el parante de mostrador sin costo. ¿Te gustaría que probemos una semana para ver cómo reaccionan tus clientes?"*

---

## 🗣️ In-Person 2-Minute Elevator Pitch (English)

> **You**: *"Hey! Great coffee today. Quick question: I noticed you use paper stamp cards. Do you find customers lose them or leave them at home a lot?"*  
> **Owner/Barista**: *"All the time. People have three different half-stamped cards in their wallets."*  
> **You**: *"Exactly. I'm a software developer and I built an open-source tool called **Alpacard**. It gives your customers a digital punch card right on their phone—no App Store download required. They just point their camera at a counter QR code and their card opens up instantly.  
> I'm setting up a free pilot for 3 local shops in the neighborhood to use it **100% free forever**. I can set up your logo and brand colors and bring you a printed counter stand this week at zero cost. Would you be open to trying it out for a week to see if your regulars like it?"*

## 📱 WhatsApp / Instagram DM Outreach Template

If you can't visit in person, message local coffee shops on Instagram or WhatsApp:

### Spanish:
> *"¡Hola equipo de [Nombre del Café]! 👋 Soy cliente habitual de su local y me encanta su café. Les escribo porque desarrollé un sistema de fidelización digital de código abierto (Alpacard) pensado para cafeterías de especialidad, para reemplazar las tarjetas de papel que los clientes siempre pierden.  
> Los clientes no tienen que descargar ninguna app pesada; solo escanean un QR en la barra con la cámara de su celular y acumulan sellos digitales.  
> Estamos seleccionando 3 cafeterías de la zona para habilitarles el sistema **100% gratis**. Si les interesa, les dejo una demo interactiva aquí: https://alpacard.gabo.lat/demo  
> ¡Me encantaría llevarles un parante de acrílico listo para probarlo sin ningún compromiso! Un abrazo."*

### English:
> *"Hi [Coffee Shop Name] team! 👋 Regular customer here, love your coffee. I’m reaching out because I built an open-source digital punch card platform (Alpacard) specifically for local indie shops to replace paper cards that customers always lose.  
> Customers don’t need to download any app; they just scan a QR code on your counter with their regular camera and collect digital stamps on their phone.  
> We’re offering a free pilot for 3 local shops with zero fees forever. You can check out an interactive demo here: https://alpacard.gabo.lat/demo  
> I’d be happy to set up your logo and bring you a printed counter stand to test out anytime. Let me know if you'd be interested! Cheers."*

---

## 🖨️ How to Prepare the Free Pilot Setup:
1. Go to `https://alpacard.gabo.lat/businesses` and create a business profile for them with their logo and brand colors.
2. In `Campaigns`, create a campaign (e.g. *"10th Coffee Free"*).
3. Click **"Print Counter Poster"** and print it on heavy cardstock or put it in a \$2 transparent acrylic display stand from a local stationery store.
4. Deliver it to the shop counter!
