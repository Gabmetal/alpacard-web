# 🗓️ Alpacard Free Marketing & Community Launch Calendar

This 7-day tactical calendar outlines step-by-step actions to drive organic traffic, attract open-source contributors, recruit beta testers, and onboard your first 3–5 real coffee shops or stores without spending a single dollar.

---

## 📌 Daily Checklist

### Day 1 (Monday): Developer & Open-Source Community Kickoff
- **Target Platforms**: `r/selfhosted` & GitHub
- **Optimal Time**: 08:30 – 10:30 AM EST (peak US/EU browsing)
- **Actions**:
  1. Post to `r/selfhosted` using [01_REDDIT_SELFHOSTED.md](01_REDDIT_SELFHOSTED.md).
  2. Pin an issue or discussion on GitHub inviting contributors.
  3. Respond to every comment within 15 minutes to boost Reddit's ranking algorithm.
- **Goal**: 100+ visitors to `alpacard.app`, 10–25 GitHub stars, 2–3 new collaborator inquiries.

---

### Day 2 (Tuesday): Technical Community & Hacker News
- **Target Platforms**: Hacker News (`Show HN`) & `r/golang`
- **Optimal Time**: 08:00 – 09:30 AM EST (crucial for Hacker News front page)
- **Actions**:
  1. Submit "Show HN" using [03_HACKERNEWS_SHOW_HN.md](03_HACKERNEWS_SHOW_HN.md).
  2. Post architecture deep dive to `r/golang` using [02_REDDIT_GOLANG_REACT.md](02_REDDIT_GOLANG_REACT.md).
  3. Monitor comments and answer technical architecture questions (RabbitMQ, Go concurrency, anti-replay tokens).
- **Goal**: High-credibility tech feedback, PR submissions, and self-hosted installs.

---

### Day 3 (Wednesday): Dev.to / Hashnode Technical Publication
- **Target Platforms**: Dev.to & Hashnode
- **Optimal Time**: 10:00 AM EST
- **Actions**:
  1. Publish the full markdown article from [04_DEV_TO_ARTICLE.md](04_DEV_TO_ARTICLE.md) on Dev.to.
  2. Cross-post to Hashnode and LinkedIn with tags `#opensource`, `#golang`, `#react`, `#webdev`.
  3. Share the post link in relevant Discord channels (e.g., Gophers, Reactiflux, Indie Hackers).
- **Goal**: Long-term SEO search indexing and developer interest.

---

### Day 4 (Thursday): Beta Testing & Cross-Device QA Hunt
- **Target Platforms**: `r/SideProject`, `r/AlphaAndBetaUsers`, `r/testmyapp`
- **Optimal Time**: 11:00 AM – 01:00 PM EST
- **Actions**:
  1. Post the tester invitation from [05_REDDIT_TESTERS_QA.md](05_REDDIT_TESTERS_QA.md).
  2. Ask users to test the camera QR scanner on different iOS and Android devices.
  3. Log bugs directly as GitHub issues.
- **Goal**: Edge-case testing across varied camera resolutions and mobile browser quirks.

---

### Day 5 (Friday): Hyper-Local Guerrilla Merchant Onboarding
- **Target**: 3 Local Neighborhood Coffee Shops / Bakeries / Food Trucks
- **Optimal Time**: Mid-afternoon (03:00 – 04:30 PM, slow period for coffee shops when owners are approachable)
- **Actions**:
  1. Print 2 sample counter QR stands using the "Print Counter Poster" feature in `CampaignsPage.tsx`.
  2. Visit your favorite local indie coffee shop or bakery.
  3. Use the conversation script from [06_LOCAL_MERCHANT_PITCH.md](06_LOCAL_MERCHANT_PITCH.md).
  4. Offer to configure their brand colors and logo on the spot for free.
- **Goal**: 1–2 real local merchants displaying the Alpacard QR code on their checkout counter.

---

### Day 6 (Saturday): Directory Listings (SEO & Discovery)
- **Target Platforms**: AlternativeTo.net, BetaList, LaunchingNext
- **Actions**:
  1. Submit Alpacard to **AlternativeTo.net** as an open-source alternative to *Stamp Me*, *Punchh*, *CandyBar*, and *Loyverse* using [07_PRODUCT_HUNT_AND_DIRECTORIES.md](07_PRODUCT_HUNT_AND_DIRECTORIES.md).
  2. Submit to BetaList and LaunchingNext for free queued listings.
- **Goal**: Passive inbound traffic from users searching for loyalty card alternatives.

---

### Day 7 (Sunday): Retrospective & Community Feature Review
- **Actions**:
  1. Review all GitHub issues created during the week.
  2. Thank every contributor and tester publicly on GitHub discussions.
  3. Tag new `good first issue` tickets for incoming contributors.
  4. Push community-requested fixes.
